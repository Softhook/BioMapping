OPA2333 Resolution Edge

~1.1 μVpp Noise Floor
Because op-amp noise (1.1 μV) is below the ADS1115 LSB (7.81 μV), the system is 100% quantization-limited. Resolves down to 0.33 nS on dry skin.

MCP602 1/f Flicker Impact

~7.0 μVpp Pink Noise
Flicker noise in the sub-Hz window rivals the ADC step, degrading effective resolution by ~1.4 ENOB bits and blurring subtle micro-arousals (<1.5 nS).

OPA2333 Thermal Invariance

0.05 μV/°C Max Drift
Over a 35°C outdoor swing (0°C winter walk to 35°C hot sun), total baseline shift is < 0.1 nS. Completely invisible to downstream EDA algorithms.

MCP602 Thermal Wander

2.5 μV/°C Typ Drift
Over 35°C, thermal drift causes ~5.5 to 11.0 nS of artificial baseline shift. A sudden sunlight transition triggers a false tonic slope!
